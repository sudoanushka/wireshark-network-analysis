1.Interface:

-Source: 2401:4900:8845:b38d:...

3. TCP information: three-way handshake
-SYN (Packet 87): client requests a connection.
-SYN, ACK (Packet 88): server acknowledges and responds.
-ACK (Packet 89): client confirms the connection.
