DNS Traffic — Screenshot Analysis

The Wireshark capture shows DNS traffic filtered using the "dns" display filter. The traffic involves 
the hosts "192.168.x.x" and "192.168.y.y".

The selected packet is a DNS query originating from "192.168.x.x" and directed to "192.168.y.y". 
At the transport layer, the packet uses UDP with source port "34714" and destination port "53", 
which is the standard DNS service port.

The packet details show the protocol layers as Ethernet II, IPv4, UDP, and DNS. The capture also 
contains DNS responses in the opposite direction, from "192.168.y.y" to "192.168.x.x".

Several DNS records are visible in the capture, including A, AAAA, and CNAME records. The traffic
includes DNS lookups for domains such as "www.youtube.com", "aus5.mozilla.org", "googleads.g.doubleclick.net",
and Mozilla-related CDN domains.

This capture demonstrates how Wireshark can be used to examine DNS communication, identify the systems involved,
determine the transport protocol and ports being used, and inspect DNS record types at packet level.

Key Observation:
The selected packet follows this communication pattern:

"192.168.x.x:34714 → UDP → 192.168.y.y:53 → DNS Query"

The corresponding responses visible in the capture demonstrate the query-response nature of DNS communication.
